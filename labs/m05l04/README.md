## Exercises

Open exercises from the lesson, to try on your own. They have no answer files: work them out, and use the labs above as reference.

### Close the gaps, then check the clock

1. Add a third person to the sev3 chain in severity.toml; predict when they would be paged.
2. Make the incident commander acknowledge at 02:26; predict who is paged next and when.
3. In deadlines.py move awareness to 23:30 on Sat 24 Oct; predict both 72-hour deadlines.
4. Write a two-sentence customer holding statement using only facts confirmed in hour one.

> **Hint:** Each person gets escalate_after_min to answer; a deadline spanning the clock change moves an hour.

## Check yourself

- In the sev1 run, why was the CISO never paged even though they are in the chain?
- Why should the incident commander avoid doing hands-on investigation during the incident?
- The team discusses the breach in the company's usual chat tool. What risk does that create, and what should the plan say instead?
- Awareness is Friday 16:30 CEST. Why is the 72-hour GDPR deadline 15:30 on Monday rather than 16:30?
